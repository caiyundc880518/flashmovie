***\*Chapter 2:\*******\*Scripting:\****

1. Writer:

Writer is the NPC who gives your script charge or no charge.So,There exist a Script Market which you can buy some written script for your film.

 

Also You can build a school to prompt your own writer whose script can be sold by you or can be selected by you to make a film.

 

2. Script:

You can hire some part-time writer to offer your script.You also can order your own writer to finish script.

 

Script has Fellow attribute:

***\*Type\****:Like Comedy,Horror,Action,Love,etc.

***\*Story Point\****:defines the difficulty of your film making.

***\*Market Potentials\****:defines the potentials of final sales.

***\*Fame Point\****:More Point,More Famous D&A will contact you.

***\*Trend\****:Gives your film buff on the right trend.

***\*Scale\****:defines your team size and your film size.Film size is defined by the number of Stage.

***\*Script-Requirement:\****Gender of actors,Age of Actors,Experience of Actor,some stage style should match to this script.

 

3.Script can be sold on the Script Market.So if your company is lack of fund.This is a way to help you.

 

4.Post Scripting Buff for your writer:While a script is finished by your writer,your writer’s ability will be improved,And your writer will level up when the ability has been improved to a specific value.

 

***\*Building A Tour Team\*******\*:\****

1. How to build a Film Team

When your preparation is done,you must build a team to shoot a film.

 

2.You can hire part time workers into your team,Also you can organize your own employees to build a team.One Team is composed by Fellow Roles:

l Producer:You can use a producer to help you find more valuable script,movie-group.Also a good producer can help you save the cost.

 

l Directors:a director is a member of the filming team.He can help you to lead the team.He will define both MP and AP in the end.Director has many attributes,one of them is Art or Market which can do the things that we mentioned above.

 

l Actors:Also the members of team Whose Art or Market will help the film to get the MP or AP.

 

l Writer:We mentioned in Script Chap.

 

l Shooter:This one is a member of the team.He controls the camera,so A good shoot will give some buff or negative buff to this film.We will use a mini game for player to control the opportunity of the buff happens.He also has Art and Market attributes which will influence the final cut.

 

l Editor:This one help your film to be more reasonable and more watchable.Edit will help your film to be more friendly to the audience.A marketable edit will help your film to make more money and more fame.An art-able edit gives your buff on the art side.

 

l Technician:Tech will help to upgrade your device or shoot skill or VFX skill or film skill.High level skill will cost your more money but will also let you to make bigger film to make bigger money.

 

l Market:Market Can help your to expand your target Market and help you to find more Good Publisher,more Good Cinema and something like Film Sale which means this people will help you make more money.

 

l Assistant:Help you do some often work.Like help training,Calculating Financial Report,Summary Season Report etc.

 

3.Every Worker Group has Assistant For You:They can help you to find the most suitable roster for your film.
